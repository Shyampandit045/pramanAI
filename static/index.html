❓ Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

Why does a genuine quotation return "Quote Not Found"?
"Quote Not Found" indicates the quotation does not appear in the specific signed judgment record verified. This does not mean the quotation is fabricated. It may derive from oral remarks from the bench, separate concurring/dissenting opinions published in alternative reporters, or interim procedural orders.
How does the engine distinguish Exact Match from Modified Quote?
Exact Match requires 100% verbatim textual and sequence alignment. Modified Quote identifies passages where AI models have altered vocabulary, omitted clauses, or introduced minor hallucinations while preserving some original words.
What causes an "Inconclusive" result?
Inconclusive occurs when the document contains scanned photocopies without an accessible digital text layer, or when OCR quality is too degraded to ensure judicial accuracy.
